How It Works

Three steps to
making an impact.

Whether you're donating equipment or receiving it, the process is simple, secure, and built on trust.

Create Your Account

Donors sign up freely. Lalíderes verify their identity with their unique LALA verification code — ensuring authenticity.

Connect & Browse

Donors list available equipment. Lalíderes share their needs. The platform matches them intelligently.
